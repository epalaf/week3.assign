*This Website will focus on Student Life, The Culinary Program, as well as the Childcare Program

Student Life and Housing

Students will be able to live on campus dorms, for $5000 a year (before scholarship funds)

While living on campus, Students have access to the Recreation Center and the Rosethorn Library

The Dining Hall is open to Students at 10AM, Culinary Students being allowed into the Kitchen at 8AM

Students involved in a Program receive $2000 in Housing Funds each year

*We hope you make yourself at home at Rosewood!

Culinary Program

Culinary Students have the opportunity to work alongside local restaurants

During the duration of the program, you will recieve funds for your housing

In completion of a year in the program, you'll receive a service credit

*Program Participants are allowed into Dining Hall 2 hours early!

Childcare Program

Childcare Students have the opportunity to work alongside local daycares, orphanages, and foster homes

During the duration of the program, you will recieve funds for your housing

In completion of a year in the program, you'll receive a service credit

*Help raise our future!
